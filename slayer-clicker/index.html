How to play

  • Click the demon to attack. Each zone needs 10 kills to unlock the next one.
  • Every 5th zone holds a boss. Beat it within the time limit or you'll be pushed back a zone.
  • Hire Tanjiro to boost your clicks, and hire the other slayers to attack automatically (DPS).
  • Hero upgrades (I–V) unlock at levels 10, 25, 50, 75 and 100. From level 200, every 25 levels gives x4 DPS.
  • Raise your Slayer Rank by slaying demons to unlock breathing skills.
  • Keyboard: 1–7 skills, A toggle auto-progress, ←/→ change zone.
