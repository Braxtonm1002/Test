Our story

A little shop with a big sweet tooth

The Fudge Kettle was born in April 2020, and our first brick-and-mortar shop opened its doors in Avon on August 22, 2020.

On May 1, 2023, the Grady family took over the kettle, and they've kept the same long-time family fudge recipe going strong, while adding a freeze-dried candy operation right in the store.

Whether you stop in for a slice of fudge or a bag of retro candy, we're always happy to see you.
